# Privacy Policy

This document explains how Consolidator handles personal data collected through the application and backend API.

## Data We Collect
- Account details such as username and email address when you register.
- Payment tokens and card metadata required to process transactions.
- Diagnostic logs used to improve the service.

## How We Use Data
- To provide the Consolidator services and maintain user accounts.
- To process payments and manage stored value cards.
- To troubleshoot issues and enhance security.

## Data Sharing
We do not sell user data. Information may be shared with payment processors and service providers only as required to operate the platform.

## Your Rights
You may request access to or deletion of your personal data at any time. Contact the project maintainers to exercise these rights.

## Contact
For privacy-related inquiries, open an issue on this repository or email the maintainers listed in `package.json`.
